# Thesis_of_the_dead

Run the game with `npm run dev`. Enter the green portal after The Freshman Woods to reach The Sophomore Room. After the fourth checkpoint, watch the Dean disappear into the exit portal, then walk onto the landing and follow him into Chapter 3, The Final Encounter.
In The Freshman Woods, every yellow orb releases two zombies from the red portal opposite the green exit (16 total). After collecting all eight orbs, follow the Dean to the green portal and walk into it. Your character shrinks and disappears before Chapter 2 loads automatically. Crossing the woods or desert boundary returns you just inside the edge, facing opposite your direction of travel, with an on-screen notice.

The Sophomore Room begins at a red portal: the Dean emerges first, then you follow him down a chase corridor. There are four puzzle sections in order: sweeping slicing disks, six moving platforms over spikes, a 40-metre retracting spike corridor, and the memory tiles at the far end. The Dean clears the saws during the entrance cinematic, then runs through all six bridge platforms and the retracting spikes while you play, and waits on the first memory tile. Eight extra metres of safe floor after both the blades and bridge give you room to walk before the next challenge; the bridge jump gaps remain the same. The landing decks are fourteen metres wide, matching the blade corridor. Only the final memory tiles require you to remember his route. Checkpoint retries keep the entire room, walls and red entrance portal at their original positions, skip the portal emergence, and resume in the safe area after the saws if you fall on the bridge, on the safe memory starting platform if you fail the first route, or at your saved memory tile for later routes.

The Dean waits in the center after emerging from the red portal, then weaves around the moving blades, timing each dodge to their sweep without stopping between blades. Your selected camera stays on your character while he demonstrates the saws and bridge and waits on the first memory tile. He moves at the restored chase pace of 12 units per second, with matching run animation and jump timing. Bridge landing positions and arc height stay consistent. In the disk corridor, press **Q** while moving to roll through a blade, or read its sweep and pass through the opening. On the moving bridge, **Space** jumps between platforms. There is no safety grate or catch floor beneath the bridge: missing a jump drops you into the spike traps. Landing on the underground pit spikes kills instantly, including during a roll or damage cooldown, without repeated combat hit reactions. Retracting floor spikes remove 20 health per hit through the combat health system, with a 0.8-second cooldown. Retry resumes at the checkpoint after the blades.

The room has two columns, indexed as `tiles[column][row]`. Every new run generates different routes, including the starting column and sideways turns. The room grows to fit the generated course. Watch the Dean demonstrate each route, then repeat his landings in the same order. The demonstrated tiles turn grey when your turn begins; only the destination stays green.

The dark room has continuous skull walls using the supplied tileable color, normal, roughness and occlusion maps. The walls have a dark finish and consistent skull scale without panel frames, bands or pipes. Textured dungeon wall torches replace the electric fixtures throughout the room, leaving the steel blade support anchors clear. Overlapping torch pairs, their fire effects and their light sources are removed together. Their fires have turbulent tongues, bright cores, orange edges, thin smoke and rising embers. Warm light originates inside each flame, falls off with distance and flickers in time with the flame and glowing coals. Distant torches keep illuminating the room while a fixed eight-light pool supplies nearby lighting and two local shadow maps. Shadows fade at torch row boundaries without changing the light energy; starting the Dean's demonstration never switches the room's light sources. Timed tiles develop cracks before breaking into falling pieces; wrong tiles break immediately. Falling reveals the spike bed below, and the first contact with its spike tips kills instantly. The final checkpoint remains safe while the Dean enters the animated portal. Entering afterward shows your character shrinking into the opening, including in first person, before the next chapter loads automatically.

The original textured model and license are served from `public/assets/models/dungeon-wall-torch/`.

Every walkable floor uses [Floor material](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/floor-material-fb32b41a61f447a8bb4e6218d7920a02) by [DJMaesen](https://sketchfab.com/bumstrum), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The same stone color, normal, roughness and occlusion maps cover the blade corridor, moving bridge platforms, memory tiles and entry/exit landings at a consistent scale. Memory route highlights and tile breakage remain visible over the stone finish. Maps and the original license are served from `public/assets/models/floor-material/`.

The entire pit uses [Spike Trap 01](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/spike-trap-01-4022678cac214fd2963894aa152fc6f2) by [Nichgon](https://sketchfab.com/nichgon), under its supplied Sketchfab Standard license. Small instanced batches keep the repeating trap panels efficient; the contact height is calculated from the loaded model's spike tips. The model, textures and original license are served from `public/assets/models/spike-trap-01/`.

After the bridge, an eight-metre safe landing checkpoints the retracting spike corridor. Nine rows rise from actual holes with recessed steel sleeves in a fixed sequence: rows 1/4/7, then 2/5/8, then 3/6/9, repeating every 7.2 seconds. Amber socket rims warn before each bank rises; the spikes retract completely below the stone floor. The Dean runs through the spike corridor without waiting at its rows, then waits at the first memory tile. Entering the retracting spike corridor starts a finite ambush of twelve Basic and Chubby zombies in four waves of three. Each wave alternates left and right jumps from the underground pit onto the corridor edges; arrivals are staggered, and the next wave waits until the current one is defeated. Zombies can cross the raised spikes and pursue you across the memory landing. When you jump off that landing, they reach its visible edge and idle there. Jumping onto a memory tile before the puzzle begins breaks it immediately; if it is the Dean's tile, he jumps to the next tile as the shared tile breaks, dropping you into the lethal pit. Clear all twelve, then approach the memory entrance to start the demonstration. If already at the entrance when the last zombie dies, step back and approach again. Cleared waves stay cleared on a checkpoint retry. Normal kill scores and ammunition drops apply. Use `http://127.0.0.1:5173/?level=2&section=spikes` to start at this checkpoint directly.

There are four routes with 4, 6, 8 and 10 jumps. The larger tiles leave room to take a few steps before jumping. Tile timers shorten from 3.2 seconds to 2.3 seconds as the Dean's demonstrations speed up. The Dean waits in the far corner of each checkpoint, leaving its center clear for your landing. Wrong landings and expired tiles collapse into the pit. Checkpoint retries keep the same generated course and show the unfinished route again.

- WASD: move relative to your view.
- Shift: sprint in every level, including the puzzle corridor.
- Space: jump in every level. Release and press again for another jump.
- Q: roll in every level. Release and press again for another roll.
- Left click: shoot or attack with the equipped weapon.
- 1: open or close the weapon inventory in every level, including Level 2. Move the mouse to highlight a weapon and click to equip it.
- 2–6: equip SMG, pistol, dual pistols, sword or dagger directly. With dual pistols, left click fires both and right click fires one.
- C: switch between third person and first person.
- R: retry the current checkpoint in the room.
- Esc: pause; click to resume.
- Gamepad (optional, enable in Options): left stick move, right stick look, LB attack (shoot a firearm or swing a melee weapon), RB sprint, RT also fires (LT single pistol), A jump, B roll, L3 gun bash, X weapon wheel, Y camera, d-pad cycles weapons — and while the wheel is open it moves the picker around the ring, A (or a click) equips the highlighted weapon and B closes the wheel. Start pauses and resumes. Menus: left stick or d-pad up/down to highlight, A to choose, B to go back (Levels, Options and Credits return to the main menu, and B in the pause screen resumes). Long panels such as Credits scroll with the stick or d-pad while the highlight stays in view, and on the Options screen left/right adjusts the focused control — sliders slide, toggles check or clear, and dropdowns step through their choices. The Level 1 field notes and on-screen hints switch to gamepad buttons whenever a controller is connected. The choice is saved.

The same black bars used for the Chapter 3 boss encounter frame player portal entrances and exits in all chapters. Both bars use one shared height and appear at their full size immediately. Portal entrances and exits travel steadily at the same 12-unit-per-second pace as the Dean's blade run; their duration follows the actual travel distance, with only a short settling interval on exit. In Level 2, the bars and input lock remain through the Dean's blade run, ending as soon as he passes the final blade. You can then move, sprint, jump, roll, shoot and change weapons while he continues across the bridge and spikes. Checkpoint retries skip the entrance cinematic. Movement pauses for the memory demonstrations, and your checkpoint remains safe until your turn starts. Tile timers also pause when the game is paused.

Level 1 opens with Shaun waking on the ground and standing up before control returns. Dreamy motion blur is enabled by default; toggle it in Options from the main menu or Settings on the pause screen. The choice is saved, and menus and HUD remain sharp. The same Options/Settings screen has a Graphics Quality preset: **Low** (for 4GB RAM PCs), **Medium** (for 8GB) and **High** (best visuals, 16GB+). Lower tiers cut render resolution, shadow quality and motion blur so the game runs smoothly on weaker machines, and the choice is saved.

Use `npm test` for the gameplay and physics checks, and `npm run build` for the production build.

To test Chapter 2 directly in Git Bash, run `npm run dev -- --host 127.0.0.1 --port 5173 --strictPort` and open `http://127.0.0.1:5173/?level=2`, then click **Start Game**. Keep the Git Bash terminal running while you play. Press **R** to retry from the current puzzle checkpoint.

Shaun carries the kit's SMG and can equip a pistol, dual pistols, sword or dagger. His hands rest until he aims or fires; dual pistols raise both arms together. The sword has a three-click combo: a basic slash, a straight forward stab and a full 360-degree spinning slash. The stab aligns the blade with your aim and extends directly forward; the spin turns the body once while the camera stays steady. Queued attacks blend directly out of the previous follow-through; the weapon fully settles only when the combo stops. Each click queues exactly one attack; holding the button adds none. One, two or three clicks stop after that many swings. Click within 0.75 seconds of the previous click to continue the combo; a later click starts again with the basic slash. Rolling, changing weapons or opening inventory cancels queued attacks. The dagger stabs quickly at close range. Bullets visibly travel from each barrel and collide with enemies or cover along their paths. Each fresh level/retry starts with 30 shared bullets; carry up to 120. Every second defeated zombie drops a green ammo box with 12 bullets, collected by walking over it. Press **F** for a short gun strike with a firearm; clicking with an empty gun also bashes. Melee weapons need no ammunition and cannot hit through walls. The equipped weapon remains visible in first person.

The Final Encounter is a sunlit desert settlement built with the supplied Zombie Kit desert assets. Four mud houses each have 100 health and four arched doorways. One zombie walks out of each doorway, staggered by half a second. Every zombie killed while its hut still stands calls four reinforcements; destroy the huts to stop the waves. Click to shoot: houses show their health when aimed at or hit, crack and shed roof sections at 75%, 50% and 25%, then collapse into persistent rubble at zero. Surviving zombies remain in combat when their house falls. Houses appear as amber squares on the minimap.

You enter the Final Encounter through a red portal at the south end. Only the player emerges; the Dean holds the degree on the raised platform at the north end. The camera returns to your selected view between the player and portal, facing the arena. Enemies and the one-minute clearing timer wait until the entrance finishes, then pause with the game. Clear every hut and zombie before 01:00 runs out. At timeout, a close-up shows the Dean laughing, raising the degree above his head, where it ignites by itself; fire consumes the parchment into charred ash before the retry screen appears. The supplied laugh from `Zombie_Kit/Sounds` plays in full at its original speed and pitch; its decoded duration, audio clock and waveform drive the laugh animation, including pause/resume. He starts raising the degree during the last second of the laugh; the degree ignites and the burning sound begins when the recording ends. Flame light, smoke, embers and quiet paper crackle follow the burning edge. Once every hut and zombie is gone, the timer stops and a cleared-arena checkpoint is saved. A gold ring marks the arena center. Retries from this checkpoint preserve the collapsed huts, skip the portal entrance and clearing phase, and place you near the center with at least 45 bullets. Enter it to summon the Dean from his platform. A letterboxed cutscene follows him to the center, then restores your selected camera before combat. Nearby, he uses double jabs, a sweeping hook and a charging uppercut. At range, he uses a telegraphed fist slide, a shadow-marked jump that ends in a ground-cracking butt slam, rush punches and a ground smash that sends rocks toward the player. Below 40% health he glows red, moves and attacks faster, and leaves very little recovery between moves. Press Q to roll away from attacks; a standing roll goes forward. The SMG has unlimited ammunition during active Dean combat. Defeating him triggers a letterboxed transformation: he falls, rises, swells, disappears inside sand-colored dust, and is replaced by the supplied Monster as the dust disperses. The larger, translucent dust cloud clears over 6.5 seconds. The camera and controls then return for the Monster final boss. He throws the actual degree through your position, curves it beyond you, and aims a separate return pass from behind before catching it; either pass can hit, and a return hit does not stop the catch. His other attack is three fast jumping ground smashes, each landing at a different position and angle, sending physical rock waves roughly 50 metres across the arena. Read the arm wind-up and the visible degree and rocks: there are no attack markers or attack-name warnings. The degree stays gripped during jumps and smashes. Bullets and melee damage the Monster, the SMG remains unlimited, and defeating him completes Level 3. Retries after the Dean restart at the transformation checkpoint. R restarts the current checkpoint; before clearing the arena it restarts the entrance and one-minute challenge. Desert asset credits and original licenses are in `public/assets/models/desert/`.
