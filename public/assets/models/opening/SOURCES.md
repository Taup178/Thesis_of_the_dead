Opening furniture from the project's Zombie_Kit folder, adapted with 1024px textures and grounded/scaled scene transforms. The bed frame is 2.6 game metres long, with a widened mattress, beside the 2.5 metre couch. It is turned toward the establishing camera and positioned with clearance for its bedside table. Original green bedding, pale pillows and wood textures are preserved, with diffuse nonmetallic bed materials. The supplied lamp's emissive material is disabled so the entire scene uses one warm spotlight, positioned to illuminate both pieces of furniture. The tiny clock's transmission is disabled to avoid an additional full-scene render pass.
