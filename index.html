SMG and pistol ammo are separate. Single and dual pistols share pistol rounds. Ammo pickups refill your equipped gun's supply (SMG rounds while holding a melee weapon). Pickups, checkpoints and portals activate when you walk into them. Follow on-screen prompts for story actions.

Graphics

Live settings

Lower for more speed; above 100% renders extra pixels for a sharper image. .

Higher resolution adds definition to shadows from trees, buildings and characters.

Keeps the ground and angled surfaces sharp as they stretch into the distance.

Smooths jagged edges on trees, rooftops and character silhouettes.

Lighting & effects

Adds depth where surfaces meet, under foliage and around objects. Higher GPU cost.

A soft glow around portals, fire and bright lights.

Richer contrast, warmer highlights and subtle darkening at the screen edges.

Dreamlike motion trails during intense moments. Turn off for a crisper view.

Display

Settings save automatically. Expensive post effects stay off during cutscenes to keep playback smooth.
