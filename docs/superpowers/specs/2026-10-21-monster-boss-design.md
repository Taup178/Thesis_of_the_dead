# Monster final boss

## Follow-up combat tuning and added moves

Later user requests extend the original two-attack scope below. Smash jumps now track the player until late descent, with landing destinations fixed at takeoff; range is capped at 20 metres, flight takes 0.5–0.7 seconds, takeoff takes 0.16 seconds and recovery takes 0.2 seconds. Attack order varies by distance. Player rolls have 0.1 seconds of recovery and a 0.22-second input buffer.

The Monster now also uses a 0.95-second close-range spinning punch, with a full turn matching the player's spinning sword attack and one 24-damage contact from the actual swept right fist. It pivots on a supporting foot and retains the degree. A separate Dean-style jumping body slam uses a 0.22-second takeoff, a committed 1.05-second ballistic flight and a seated landing. Its 9.5-metre radius deals 32 damage once on impact; dodging or jumping high enough avoids it. Ground cracks, stones and dust appear after landing. Native animation samples preserve the arm joints for both new attacks.

The existing Level 3 Monster reveal becomes an active second boss phase after the transformation and camera return. Preserve the Dean encounter and the current opening animation changes. Reuse Three.js, the supplied Monster rig, the existing degree, player weapons, dodge invulnerability and damage feedback. Build only the two requested attacks. No painted attack areas, aim strips, warning rings, targeting lights or attack-name notifications; anticipation comes from the creature's physical preparation and the visible projectiles.

## Degree boomerang

The Monster winds its degree-holding left arm back, twists through the chest, releases the actual scroll and follows through. The degree spins and banks along a continuous outward curve through the player's sampled torso position, continues beyond the player, then makes a rounded return. At the turnaround the return path samples the player's new position once, approaching it from the far side before returning to the open hand. Each leg can damage once independently, so dodging the outbound pass does not cancel the return threat. No continuous homing or instant reversal. Preserve the scroll's scale and finger grip, and attach it again at the catch without a visible teleport. Damage uses swept projectile collision and accounts for vertical separation. Rolling and moving out of its path can evade either pass.

## Three ground smashes

A three-impact sequence with a fast jump to a different position before every smash, including the first. For each jump, sample the player's position and choose a landing roughly 4.8 metres away, approaching from three different angles (-45, 0 and +45 degrees relative to the combo's initial direction). Cap long jumps at 14 metres, keep landings inside the arena and commit each landing before takeoff. Flight uses the same gravity as the player, with 0.6–0.82 seconds airborne, a short takeoff brace and 0.32 seconds of landing recovery. Turn in the air rather than sliding the feet around on the ground.

The Monster lifts both arms on a curved path in front of the shoulders, jumps, then drives the fists down as it lands. The shoulder, elbow and wrist rotations come from the supplied artist-authored animation, smoothly blended between its native wind-up, strike and rest poses; procedural arm IK is not used. Feet alone are constrained to the landing contacts. Ground contact targets must account for the actual deformed hand and held paper geometry, not just wrist joint positions. The degree stays fitted to the left palm throughout every jump and smash, leaving the hand only during the throw. Each impact releases its own visible rock wave with dust, cracks and tumbling debris from the actual fist contact. Cracks appear with the impact/front, never as a pre-attack indicator. Waves travel approximately 50 metres (the Dean's waves reach about 15 metres), keep their initial direction and allow sideways dodges, rolls and sufficiently high jumps. Each wave can damage once.

Alternate these two attacks so both can be tested. The degree continues to the boss's hand even when it hits the player on its return. Keep SMG ammunition unlimited during this second boss phase, expose a Monster health bar, and accept both firearm and melee damage. Killing the Monster stops hazards and completes the encounter. Player death also clears hazards. Retries after defeating the Dean restart at the transformation/reveal checkpoint rather than replaying the Dean fight. Death, retry and level disposal restore the degree's ownership and release new resources without disposing shared actor assets twice. Tests cover outbound and rear return threats, independent rolls, range, all three lanes/impacts, frame-step collision, rig contacts, damage routing, activation after camera return and cleanup.
