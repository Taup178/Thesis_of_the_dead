# Degree collection and destiny choice

After the Monster dies, his body settles on its back with relaxed limbs. Shaun approaches using his walking/running animations and arena navigation, crouches to the fallen hand, and reaches for the original degree. Ownership changes only at hand contact. The Monster's fingers open as Shaun lifts the degree, stands, and raises it in celebration.

The camera then reveals a red portal and a blue portal opening beside the Dean's stand. Controls return in third person with the degree still in Shaun's hand. The challenge HUD reads **Choose your destiny**. Walk into either portal to commit that colour and finish the encounter; the game does not choose automatically. Portal posts have collisions and the openings remain clear.

Voice is deferred to the user's next session. The two colours currently record the selected destiny and lead to the existing completion flow; further story outcomes can be authored later.
