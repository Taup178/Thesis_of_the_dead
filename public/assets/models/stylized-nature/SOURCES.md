Downloaded 2026-10-05. The original Google Drive returned “Quota exceeded” for file downloads. These GLB files are the same pack published under Quaternius's Poly Pizza account. All 12 model collections available in that bundle are included, with embedded textures. `manifest.json` preserves the mirror's model names, IDs, author and license metadata.

Level 1 uses birch, broadleaf, maple, pine and dead trees, rocks, bushes, grass and flowers. Palm trees, the second dead-tree collection and flower bushes are included for future use but are not loaded by Level 1. At runtime the gallery models are separated, grounded, resized and instanced; leaf transparency uses alpha cutouts for stable shadows.
